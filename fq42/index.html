About

It’s a quiet night in the desert. The drone sits on the flight line, with orange mesas and dark mountains behind it under a sky full of stars. That scene is what I wanted to capture here. Kill the lights and it comes alive: the sky glows blue, and the stars and Ultio Insomnis lettering glow green. Magnets are built into the back, so it snaps onto any steel surface, like a toolbox, a file cabinet, or the side of your desk.

  • Multicolor 3D-printed design with raised detail
  • Just under 8 in across
  • Glow-in-the-dark Ultio Insomnis lettering, stars and night sky
  • Arched Latin motto: Ultio Insomnis
  • Embedded magnets for mounting on steel surfaces
  • Made to order
